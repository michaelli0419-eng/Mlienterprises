What we do

Operational & management consulting

We work with owners and operators on process, reporting, and systems decisions. Engagements are scoped around a specific operational problem and end when it is solved.

Custom application development

We design and build software for the specifics of your business when off-the-shelf tools don't fit. Typical work includes internal tools, workflow systems, and integrations between the systems you already run.

02

Selected work

Production & scheduling system for a modular manufacturer

A factory running a 15-station production line was scheduling in spreadsheets. We built the system that now plans line capacity, advances modules station by station from the shop floor, and tracks every module by QR code from the line to the job site.

Manufacturing · Scheduling · Field tracking

Estimating platform replacing a spreadsheet workflow

An estimating team was rebuilding every bid by hand in Excel. We built a versioned estimating engine that goes from drawings to a bill of materials to a client-ready proposal, validated against the company's own historical bids before it replaced anything.

Estimating · BOM · Proposals

Month-end close and job-cost automation for a construction group

A controller was closing the books over weeks of manual journal entries. We automated WIP schedules, payroll allocations, and owner billing against the existing ERP — replicating the controller's exact postings rather than asking the team to change how they work.

Accounting · ERP integration · Automation
